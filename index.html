Cratebox

An offline music player for iPhone, for the songs you already own: MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV and AIFF. No adverts, no account, no subscription, and no internet connection needed.

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: dimroom.app@outlook.com. Please say which iPhone and which version of Cratebox you have (Settings in the app).

Common questions

How do I get songs in? Two ways. On the iPhone: save the files to the Files app (iCloud Drive, On My iPhone or a USB drive), then in Cratebox tap Import from Files (on the Library tab, or in Settings) and pick them. From a Mac or PC: connect the iPhone, open Finder (Mac) or the Apple Devices app (Windows), choose Files, and drag songs or whole folders onto Cratebox. Folders stay as you made them.

Does Cratebox stream or download music? No. It plays files you already have: ripped CDs, purchases from music shops that sell files, your own recordings. It cannot play songs from Apple Music or other streaming services, because those are not files you own.

Where are my songs? In Cratebox's own folder, which you can open in the Files app (On My iPhone, then Cratebox). They are ordinary files, not locked inside the app.

A song shows the wrong title or no cover. Cratebox reads the title, artist, album, track number and cover art saved inside the file. If those are missing, it uses the file name. Fix the tags with a tag editor on your computer and copy the file in again.

A song will not play. The file may be damaged, or in a format iPhone cannot play (for example WMA or OGG). Convert it to MP3, M4A or FLAC on your computer and copy it in again.

What is Cratebox Pro? A one-off purchase, not a subscription. It adds a sleep timer, playback speed from half to double, smart playlists that fill themselves by your rules, three more colour themes (Night, Moss and Ember) and saving a library backup. Everything else stays free, and restoring a backup is free.

Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling: sign in with the same Apple account, open Cratebox Pro in Settings and tap Restore purchase. You do not pay again.

Moving to a new phone: copy your music across as you first put it in. To keep your playlists, play counts and ratings too, save a library backup in Settings on the old phone (Pro) and restore it in Settings on the new one (free).

Deleting the app deletes your music. Your songs live in Cratebox's folder, so keep your own copies on a computer or in iCloud Drive before you delete the app.
