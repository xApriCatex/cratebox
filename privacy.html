Privacy policy

For the Cratebox iPhone app. Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version

Cratebox collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics, and it needs no internet connection. We never see your music or your library.

Who we are

Cratebox is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: dimroom.app@outlook.com.

Where your music is kept

Your songs are files in Cratebox's own folder on your iPhone, which you can see in the Files app. Play counts, ratings, playlists and settings are saved in Cratebox's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. If your phone makes its own backup (for example iCloud Backup), that backup includes them, under your Apple account's privacy terms.

When anything leaves your phone

Cratebox itself sends your library nowhere. It leaves the phone in two ways only: in your phone's own backups (such as iCloud Backup), if you have them turned on, under your Apple account's terms; and in a library backup you choose to save (your playlists, play counts and ratings, not the songs), which goes wherever you pick in the share sheet. What happens to it there is up to you and that app.

Buying Cratebox Pro

Cratebox Pro is a one-off purchase made through Apple. Apple handles the payment and we never see your card or Apple account details; the app only learns whether your Apple account owns Pro, and remembers that in the iPhone's keychain so Pro works offline.

Permissions

Cratebox asks for no permissions. Importing songs and restoring a backup use the iPhone's own file picker, which shows the app only the files you choose. The lock screen and Control Center show the song that is playing.

Deleting your data

Deleting a song from its menu in Cratebox deletes the file. Deleting the app removes your music, library and settings from your iPhone, except a small note in the iPhone's keychain saying whether it has Pro, which can stay until the phone is reset. Files you have saved elsewhere and your phone's own backups are not touched; delete those where they are. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Cratebox collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Your rights

Questions about privacy: dimroom.app@outlook.com. In the UK you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
